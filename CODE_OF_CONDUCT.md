# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Everyone taking part in issues, pull requests and discussions here is expected to keep to it.

In short: be respectful and welcoming, give and take feedback on the work rather than the person, and do not harass anyone.

## Reporting

If something here makes you uncomfortable, tell the maintainer, @Pancheble. For something you would rather not raise in public, use GitHub's **Report content** on the comment or issue, or [report abuse](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse) to GitHub. Reports are handled privately, and the maintainer may edit or remove content, or block people who break this code.
